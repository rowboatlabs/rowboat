# Replicas logo

- `logo.png`: `replicas-symbol-green-light.svg` (the symbol in the light-UI green, `#2F9D57`)
- `logo-dark.png`: `replicas-symbol-green.svg` (the symbol in the dark-UI green, `#3EEBA3`)

Both come from the Replicas brand page, <https://replicas.dev/brand>, downloaded on 2026-09-30 from `https://replicas.dev/brand/replicas-symbol-green-light.svg` and `https://replicas.dev/brand/replicas-symbol-green.svg`; they are byte-identical to the copies in the page's `replicas-brand-assets.zip`. The kit says to use `#3EEBA3` on dark surfaces and `#2F9D57` on light ones. Each square 360 × 360 SVG, which already includes the kit's one-cell clear space, was rendered to 128 × 128 with sharp 0.35.3 (libvips 8.18.3, librsvg 2.62.90), keeping its transparent background, with no other change. The mark belongs to Replicas Group Inc. and is used here only to identify Replicas.
