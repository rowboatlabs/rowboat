# Cursor logo

- `logo.png`: `General Logos/Cube/SVG/CUBE_2D_LIGHT.svg` (the dark cube, `#26251E`, for light backgrounds)
- `logo-dark.png`: `General Logos/Cube/SVG/CUBE_2D_DARK.svg` (the light cube, `#EDECEC`, for dark backgrounds)

Both come from Cursor's brand kit, `cursor-brand-assets.zip`, downloaded on 2026-09-30 from the "Download" link on <https://cursor.com/brand> (`https://ptht05hbb1ssoooe.public.blob.vercel-storage.com/assets/brand/cursor-brand-assets.zip`). The cube is taller than it is wide (467 × 532), so each SVG was rendered with sharp 0.35.3 (libvips 8.18.3, librsvg 2.62.90) to fit inside 128 × 128 and centred on a transparent square, with no other change. The same kit also has square app icons and avatars with a solid plate, under `App Icons/` and `Avatars/`. The mark belongs to Anysphere and is used here only to identify Cursor.
